    About this atlas

    Fauna Atlas shows which animals people have recorded on land, in rivers and lakes, in the sea and in the air. Click a spot to see the species found within a square about 150 km across, or search for an animal or a place to see where it has been seen, how high or deep it lives, and when.

    A list here is a record of what was found, not a census. Most of the Earth has hardly been surveyed, and insects, spiders and worms far less than birds and mammals, so a short list usually means few people looked. Switch on Where people have looked to see the gaps, and where nobody has recorded an animal for ten years. GBIF coarsens the positions of species at risk from collectors and poachers; this atlas shows them as they are published.

    Where the data comes from

    • Animal records, lists, maps, heights, depths and dates: GBIF, the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (each dataset keeps its own licence: CC0, CC BY or CC BY-NC). Names and classification: GBIF Backbone Taxonomy.
    • Photos, sounds, body size and lifespan: Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata (CC0, queried through QLever); where Wikidata has no photo, an openly licensed (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) iNaturalist photo from GBIF. Every photo and sound is credited.
    • Summaries: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Red List status: IUCN, through GBIF.
    • Places: Photon and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap data, ODbL), sea names from Marine Regions (CC BY 4.0).
    • Globe: NASA Blue Marble through GIBS (public domain); close up, Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX (CC BY 4.0, contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016); borders and names from OpenStreetMap through OpenFreeMap.
    • Sea migrations: MiCO, Bentley et al. (2025), data Zenodo (CC0).

    Privacy

    This site has no server of its own, no cookies and no analytics; it remembers only your language choice, in your browser. Your browser loads the data straight from the services above, so each of them sees your IP address and what you look up, as with any website. Near me uses your location only in your browser: what is sent to GBIF is the outline of the square, and the name of the place is asked from Nominatim, as for any click.

    Code: github.com/bristleconelab/fauna-atlas (MIT). Sister projects: Ocean Life Atlas (the sea in depth, with tag tracks), Seafloor Atlas.

    Atlas haqida

    Fauna Atlas odamlar quruqlikda, daryo va koʻllarda, dengizda va havoda qayd etgan hayvonlarni koʻrsatadi. Taxminan 150 km lik kvadratda topilgan turlarni koʻrish uchun biror joyni bosing yoki hayvon yoki joyni qidirib, u qayerda koʻrilgani, qanchalik balandda yoki chuqurda yashashi va qachon uchrashini biling.

    Bu yerdagi roʻyxat sanoq emas, topilgan narsalar yozuvi. Yerning katta qismi deyarli oʻrganilmagan, hasharotlar, oʻrgimchaklar va chuvalchanglar esa qush va sutemizuvchilardan ancha kam oʻrganilgan, shuning uchun qisqa roʻyxat odatda kam odam qaraganini bildiradi. Boʻshliqlarni va oʻn yildan beri hayvon qayd etilmagan joylarni koʻrish uchun Odamlar qayerni oʻrgangan qatlamini yoqing. GBIF ovchi va brakonyerlardan xavf ostidagi turlarning joylashuvini qoʻpollashtirib eʼlon qiladi; atlas ularni eʼlon qilinganidek koʻrsatadi.

    Maʼlumotlar manbasi

    • Hayvon yozuvlari, roʻyxatlar, xaritalar, balandlik, chuqurlik va sanalar: GBIF (har bir toʻplam oʻz litsenziyasida: CC0, CC BY yoki CC BY-NC). Nomlar va tasnif: GBIF Backbone Taxonomy.
    • Suratlar, ovozlar, oʻlcham va umr: Wikimedia Commons va Wikidata (CC0, QLever orqali); Wikidata da surat boʻlmasa, GBIF dagi ochiq litsenziyali (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) iNaturalist surati. Har bir surat va ovoz muallifi koʻrsatilgan.
    • Qisqa maʼlumot: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Qizil roʻyxat: IUCN, GBIF orqali.
    • Joylar: Photon va Nominatim (OpenStreetMap, ODbL), dengiz nomlari Marine Regions dan (CC BY 4.0).
    • Globus: NASA Blue Marble (GIBS); yaqindan — EOX ning Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 (CC BY 4.0); chegaralar va nomlar OpenStreetMap dan, OpenFreeMap orqali.
    • Dengiz migratsiyalari: MiCO, Bentley va boshq. (2025), maʼlumot Zenodo (CC0).
